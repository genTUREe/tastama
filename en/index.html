When was the Kazakh Khanate founded?

Example student answer1991
Correct answer 1465–1466

Two different historical events

1991 refers to Kazakhstan's independence. The founding of the Kazakh Khanate is traditionally dated to 1465–1466 and associated with Kerey and Zhanibek. Separate these events on a timeline before trying again.

What to revisit

The founding of the Kazakh Khanate: Kerey, Zhanibek and the historical context of the 15th century.

Built by students. Built for Kazakhstan.

A local challenge. A student team's ambition.

TASTAMA is a Kazakhstani student-founded EdTech startup in Astana. We're developing an AI-powered UNT preparation platform, shaped by a simple idea: a mistake can show the way forward.

Built by students from Astana's universities. University affiliations describe individual team members and do not imply an official partnership or endorsement.

  1. 01

    The problem

    A score alone doesn't always tell a student what to study next.

  2. 02

    The idea

    Connect testing, explanations and practice in one learning journey.

  3. 03

    The MVP

    Develop student and teacher interfaces and refine the core learning experience.

  4. 04

    The next steps

    Improve reliability and content, then explore pilots with students and education centers.

Privacy notice

This notice describes how this public TASTAMA website handles information. It covers the marketing website, not the learning platform.

Using the website

You can explore the website without an account. This website does not ask for student records, test responses or payment information.

Contact drafts

The contact form prepares a draft in your browser. Automatic sending is not connected. Copying or downloading a draft saves it where you choose. If a verified email address is configured later, the form can open a draft in your email app; you decide whether to send it.

Browser storage

The website stores your chosen language in your browser's local storage, where available. Form drafts are not saved in local storage. You can remove the language preference by clearing this website's browser data.

Analytics

No third-party analytics or tracking cookies are included. Interaction events are prepared locally for future integration and are not transmitted to an analytics service.

Hosting

Your browser requests this page and its assets from the website's host. The host can process technical request data, including an IP address, under its own policies.

Contact channel

A corporate contact email has not yet been verified. The website will connect a contact channel after it has been checked. Until then, prepared drafts are not delivered to the team.
